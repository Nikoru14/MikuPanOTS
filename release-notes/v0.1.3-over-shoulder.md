## New

- **Camera collision against what is on screen.** The over-the-shoulder
  camera now collides with the room as it is drawn, not with the game's
  walking floor plan:
  - it looks over low furniture (tables, chests, the dolls on a cabinet)
    instead of zooming in on Miku;
  - walking through an open doorway no longer pulls it in; closed doors,
    walls and tall furniture still stop it;
  - it no longer snaps in around the floor hole in the first area.
- **Camera collision readout** for the curious: with the overlay open,
  **Debug › Camera › Show Camera World Info** shows how far the camera may
  hang back and what stopped it.

## Fixes

## Installing

1. Download the zip for your platform and your game's region: **US** for the
   NTSC-U (USA) disc, **EU** for the PAL (EU) disc. NTSC-J discs are not
   supported.
2. Extract it, then copy `IMG_HD.BIN` and `IMG_BD.BIN` from your disc next to
   the `MikuPan` executable.
3. In game, open **Settings › Game › Camera** and set **Camera View** to
   **Over the Shoulder**.

These packages are Debug builds, as the README recommends: the Release
configuration has known issues.
