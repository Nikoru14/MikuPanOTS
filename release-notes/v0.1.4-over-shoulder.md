## New

- **Gyro aiming** (from MikuPan): while the Camera Obscura is raised, a
  controller with a gyro (DualSense, DualShock 4, Switch Pro, ...) aims it by
  being turned, alongside the stick and the mouse. **Settings › Controls ›
  Gyro** sets the sensitivity, yaw or roll to turn, and the inversions; the
  gyro's drift is learned whenever the controller lies still with the Camera
  Obscura down.
- **Gyro recenter behavior** (**Settings › Controls › Gyro**): the right half
  of the touch pad with the Camera Obscura raised holds the gyro still while
  pressed, so the controller can be brought back to a comfortable angle, and
  then:
  - **Zero** (the default): the view stays where it is.
  - **Level**: the view tips back to level.
  - **Level and back to start**: it also turns back to where Miku faced when
    she raised the camera.

## Fixes

- **Walking through a camera change no longer fights the over-the-shoulder
  camera.** Holding the stick through a door, a search or an event shot keeps
  Miku walking the way she was, so the cut never turns her round, but coming
  back to the over-the-shoulder camera that old direction used to stick until
  the stick was let go. Now it eases onto the camera's direction within a few
  frames, and steering the stick a new way hands it to the camera at once.

## Installing

1. Download the zip for your platform and your game's region: **US** for the
   NTSC-U (USA) disc, **EU** for the PAL (EU) disc. NTSC-J discs are not
   supported.
2. Extract it, then copy `IMG_HD.BIN` and `IMG_BD.BIN` from your disc next to
   the `MikuPan` executable.
3. In game, open **Settings › Game › Camera** and set **Camera View** to
   **Over the Shoulder**.

These packages are Debug builds, as the README recommends: the Release
configuration has known issues.
